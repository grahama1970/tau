# Tau Goal Summary

Tau lets a human launch and supervise a small ladder of real, goal-locked agent DAGs, from simple to durable and failure-recovering, while showing truthful progress, accepted evidence, blockers, and required human decisions in one easy-to-use interface. Humans decide what must happen. Agents propose and execute bounded work. Tau decides what counts as admissible progress.

## Canonical DAG Ladder

1. Simple linear DAG
2. Multi-step sequential DAG
3. Concurrent fan-out/fan-in DAG
4. Mixed sequential/concurrent DAG
5. Durable mixed-topology DAG
